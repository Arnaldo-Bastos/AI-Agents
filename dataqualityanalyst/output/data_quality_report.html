Data Quality Report

Executive Summary

The dataset "order-delivery-dataset" contains 100,000 rows and 23 columns with no missing or duplicate rows detected. The data quality is high with zero null or blank cells across all columns.

However, multiple geographic coordinate fields ("Restaurant_Lat", "Restaurant_Lon", "Customer_Lat", "Customer_Lon", "Driver_Lat", "Driver_Lon") exhibit informational outliers beyond the 1.5*IQR bounds, affecting roughly 14% of records per coordinate. Additionally, "Delivery_Distance_km" contains a very small proportion (0.03%) of outlying values.

Dates are stored as text but parse correctly into datetime values, flagged with medium severity for potential pre-processing. No critical errors or data corruption have been identified.

Prioritized Findings

  • INFO: Columns Restaurant_Lat, Restaurant_Lon, Customer_Lat, Customer_Lon, Driver_Lat, and Driver_Lon exhibit 14.2% (approx.) of values that fall outside the standard 1.5 * IQR bounds. These are likely geographic coordinate outliers or extreme values consistent with legitimate variation. No evidence indicates errors. Consider domain knowledge for interpretation.
  • INFO: Column Delivery_Distance_km has a very small fraction (30 records, 0.03%) of outliers beyond the IQR bounds. This low count suggests rare but potentially valid long-distance deliveries or measurement deviations rather than systemic errors.
  • MEDIUM: Order_Time and Delivery_Time are stored as text strings but parse correctly to dates. While functional, this may impair efficient time-based operations without conversion.
  • No missing or null values detected; completeness is excellent.
  • No duplicate rows present, assuring uniqueness of records.

Recommendations

  • Evaluate longitude and latitude outliers with domain expertise to determine if values reflect actual geographic spread or require trimming.
  • Convert Order_Time and Delivery_Time to datetime types to support accurate temporal analysis and avoid potential downstream errors.
  • No missing data imputation or row deletions are needed based on completeness.
  • Retain coordinate outliers for now to preserve all geographic diversity unless domain rules dictate exclusion.

Positive Observations

  • Zero missing or blank cells across all 23 columns enhances analytical confidence.
  • Complete uniqueness of identifiers ensures high data integrity.
  • The dataset exhibits strong typological consistency and no structural anomalies.
  • Effective data volume and variety enable robust modeling opportunities.

Methodology and Limitations

Data profiling was performed using statistical summaries and IQR-based outlier detection across numeric fields. Only continuous numeric columns with well-understood semantics and detected outliers were charted. Binary, categorical, identifier, and date fields were excluded from outlier charts to avoid misinterpretation.

IQR outliers are informational; they do not inherently indicate errors but signal values outside typical ranges. The meaning of geographic extremes requires expert domain knowledge for action determination.

This report does not modify data or assume business rules not explicit in the profiling results. Recommendations reflect technical best practices and measured risks only.

Actions Before Analysis

  1. Convert Order_Time and Delivery_Time columns from string to datetime type. Verify conversion success with e.g. pd.to_datetime().
  2. Review geographic coordinate outliers in Restaurant_Lat, Restaurant_Lon, Customer_Lat, Customer_Lon, Driver_Lat, and Driver_Lon to decide on retention or filtering based on domain-specific geographic boundaries.
  3. Assess records with outlying Delivery_Distance_km values by filtering Delivery_Distance_km < 0 or Delivery_Distance_km > 5.277907964423646 for validity checks; decide on inclusion or exclusion accordingly.
