Executive Summary

This dataset, containing 12,330 rows and 18 columns, exhibits generally high data completeness with no missing or blank cells detected. No null value imputations are recommended due to absence of missing data. Recommendations focus on handling duplicated rows and considering outlier impact as informed by business knowledge.

Methodology and Limitations

This data quality assessment employed automated profiling techniques to quantify missingness, duplication, uniqueness, and outlier detection via interquartile range (IQR) analysis. Duplicate rows were identified by exact matching of all column values. Numeric summaries are based on 100% of available non-null data.

Limitations include lack of domain knowledge in interpreting outliers and rare categories; these flags do not equate to errors but require business context. No validity rules or thresholds beyond IQR were applied. The report does not modify or cleanse data but provides evidence-based indicators.

Imputation recommendations are strictly limited to numeric columns with measured nulls; here none exist, so no imputation is advised. Outliers and rare categories are reported but marked as informational only.
